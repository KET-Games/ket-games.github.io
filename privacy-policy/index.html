1. Information handled by the game

Local gameplay data

JumpRift uses local save functionality to retain gameplay-related progress on your device.

This information is stored locally on your device for normal game functionality. JumpRift does not require a separate KET GAMES account.

Local save data may be removed when you clear the game's app storage or uninstall the game.

Google Play Games Services

JumpRift integrates Google Play Games Services for authentication and achievements.

The game may automatically attempt Google Play Games authentication when the service is available on your device.

Signing into Google Play Games is not required to access the core Day and Night gameplay modes.

When Google Play Games authentication succeeds, the service may provide gamer identity information, such as your Play Games player identifier and gamer profile information, to support the integration.

The game uses Google Play Games to:

  • Check and retrieve achievement information.
  • Submit achievement unlocks.
  • Associate achievement progress with the authenticated Google Play Games profile.

Players who are not authenticated can still play the core game, but Google Play Games achievement functionality may not be available.

Google Play Games Services is operated by Google. Google may automatically process service analytics and diagnostic information to maintain, troubleshoot and improve its services. Other information may be processed depending on the service features and configuration.

Google's published Play Games Services data-collection documentation describes the latest version of its SDKs. The Android build reviewed for this policy included Google Play Games Services SDK v2, version 19.0.0. Therefore, Google's latest-SDK disclosures are provided as general guidance and may not describe every detail of the SDK version included in this build. Actual data handling also depends on the SDK integration and the features used by the game.

Google handles information processed through its services under its own privacy policies.

Google privacy information:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Games data and privacy information:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3129346

Google's Play Games Services data collection documentation:
https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection

2. Information I do not intentionally collect through gameplay

In the reviewed version, JumpRift:

  • Does not require players to create a separate KET GAMES account.
  • Does not include advertisements or in-app purchases.
  • Does not request access to precise device location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • Does not use an advertising ID permission.

Google Play Games Services may process additional account, device or service information as part of Google's own platform functionality. That processing is governed by Google's policies.

3. How information is used

Information handled through the game and its integrated services is used for:

  • Saving local gameplay progress.
  • Providing Google Play Games authentication when available.
  • Retrieving and submitting achievements.
  • Maintaining and improving the stability of Google Play Games Services.

I do not sell player information or use it for advertising.

4. Data sharing

JumpRift uses Google Play Games Services for authentication and achievement functionality.

Information required by this integration may be transmitted to Google and processed by Google to provide those services.

Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy and applicable service terms.

I do not operate a separate advertising network or sell player information to third parties.

5. Data storage and retention

Local gameplay save information is stored on your device. You can remove local app data through your device's Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

Google Play Games information is handled and retained by Google under its own policies and account settings.

If you contact me by email, I will receive the email address and information you include in your message. I retain that correspondence only as reasonably necessary to respond and handle the inquiry.

6. Security

Google states that Play Games Services encrypts the relevant collected data in transit using HTTPS.

Local gameplay data is stored on your device. Please keep your device and Android account secure.

7. Your choices and data controls

You can play the core Day and Night modes without successfully signing into Google Play Games.

You can manage your Google Play Games profile, automatic sign-in preferences and associated game data through Google's account and Play Games settings.

For information about managing or deleting Google Play Games data, visit:

https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3129346

To delete Play Games data from your Google Account, Google provides a direct data deletion page:

https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646

Deleting Play Games data from a Google Account does not automatically delete information collected separately by a game developer.

Google controls the data held within its own Play Games services. I cannot directly delete information stored in your Google account.

For questions about information handled directly by JumpRift, contact me using the email address below.

8. Children and family accounts

JumpRift does not require players to submit personal details or create a separate developer account to play.

Google Play Games features are provided by Google and are subject to Google's account, age and family settings.

Parents or guardians with questions about privacy and the use of Google Play Games features may contact me.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services or its data-handling practices change.

The latest version will always be available at this page. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
